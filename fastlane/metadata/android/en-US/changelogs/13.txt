Better device handling.

The picker in the header now lists what the phone is actually connected to,
rather than every paired device — speakers, mice and laptops included. It only
falls back to name matching when nothing is connected.

Devices named "Jabra" now rank above ones that merely say "Elite", which is
also a brand of cycling trainers. And the header chip marks itself as tappable,
so earbuds you renamed stay reachable instead of looking unpaired.
