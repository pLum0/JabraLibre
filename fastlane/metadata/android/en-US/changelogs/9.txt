Fixes the Quick Settings tile showing itself as inactive while the earbuds
were connected.

The tile is bound into a fresh process every time the app has been killed, and
the current mode only ever lived in memory — so the tile knew no mode and
could not show itself as active. It is now remembered between runs.

Also hardened: the earbuds are found by address as well as by name, since
Android can report no device name yet in a fresh process.
