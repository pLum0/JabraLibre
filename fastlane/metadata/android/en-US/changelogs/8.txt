Reworked interface: three large mode buttons instead of the raw protocol log,
which moves into a collapsed Diagnostics section. New icon, light and dark
themes, German translation.

New: a Quick Settings tile that cycles ANC / HearThrough / Off in one tap,
also from the lock screen.

New: all three battery levels — left bud, right bud and charging case.

No mode is shown as applied unless the earbuds confirmed it, and the app
reconnects by itself when they come out of the case.
