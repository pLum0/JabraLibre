Reworked interface: three large mode buttons instead of a raw protocol log,
which now lives in a collapsed Diagnostics section. New icon, dark and light
themes, German translation.

New: Quick Settings tile that cycles ANC / HearThrough / Off in one tap, also
from the lock screen.

New: all three battery levels — left bud, right bud and charging case.

The app no longer claims a mode the earbuds never confirmed, says so within a
second when the buds are not connected at all, and reconnects by itself when
you take them out of the case.
