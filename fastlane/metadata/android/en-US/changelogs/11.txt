Makes the reproducible build actually verify.

0.3.3 was signed with a real key, but the signing step also re-aligned every
entry in the APK, so nobody could rebuild the exact same bytes from source.
Signing now preserves the existing alignment, and an independent rebuild of
this release reproduces the published APK exactly.

Nothing changes in the app. This one installs over 0.3.3 as a normal update.
